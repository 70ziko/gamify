Radii — pill 999 · card 18–20 · hero card 24–26 · sheet 30 top · chip 999 · icon tile 11–16 · CTA 14–18
Spacing — 4 / 7 / 10 / 14 / 17 / 20 / 24 base; screen gutter 20, sheet gutter 22

Hit targets — tab item 56×44 min · CTA height 50 · center FAB 66×66, offset −32
Borders — 1px hairline default, 1.5px for selected/active states
Component notes
Chunky buttons — solid 4–6px bottom shadow, no blur. In RN use a wrapper View with the dark tone + inner View translated −4/−6 on press (Pressable + Reanimated).
Path nodes — SVG path drawn with react-native-svg on a fixed 390×760 canvas; nodes absolutely positioned at the same coordinates. Completed = filled primary coin, chest = rounded rect, optional/review = 45°-rotated square.
Progress bars — height 6–10, fully rounded track, gradient fill via expo-linear-gradient.
Sheets — 30px top radius, backdrop at 28–32% opacity, grabber 42×5.
Tab bar — 5 slots; center is the Continue FAB (jumps to the active roadmap's current step), not a route.
Glow accent — the FAB's pulse is the only ambient animation; everything else animates only on interaction.
